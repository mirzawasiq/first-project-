LIBERTYDRIVE

An open-world sandbox in your browser

On Foot

  • WASD Move
  • Mouse Look (click to capture)
  • Arrow Keys Rotate camera
  • Right-drag Look (if mouse not captured)
  • Shift Sprint
  • Shift+Space / E BOOST
  • Space Jump
  • F Enter vehicle
  • Left Click Attack / Shoot
  • 1-4 / Q Switch weapon

In Vehicle

  • W / S Gas / Brake
  • A / D Steer
  • Space Handbrake
  • F Exit vehicle
  • Mouse / Arrows Look

Goal

  • Reach yellow markers for paid jobs.
  • Grab glowing pickups for health, armour, ammo, cash and weapons.
  • Cause chaos, raise your wanted level.
  • Evade the cops or get WASTED / BUSTED.
  • It's a sandbox — go wild.
